About AL-MUDIR

AL-MUDIR is a managed forex and digital asset platform headquartered in the UAE, delivering institutional-grade account management using ICT and Smart Money Concepts methodology across FX, gold, and crypto markets.

Our Story

Founded by traders who spent years executing at institutional desks, AL-MUDIR was created to bridge the gap between retail investors and institutional-quality execution. We recognised that most retail platforms lack the risk management, methodology discipline, and transparency that serious capital requires.

Our approach combines Inner Circle Trader (ICT) methodology with Smart Money Concepts (SMC) to identify high-probability trade setups across forex majors, gold (XAUUSD), and digital assets. Every position is managed with defined risk parameters, verifiable on-chain settlement for crypto, and 24-hour payout cycles for investment plans.

Based in the UAE with operations serving accredited investors globally, AL-MUDIR manages capital with the same discipline and accountability expected of institutional fund managers.

Trading Methodology

Our execution framework is built on two complementary institutional methodologies:

ICT — Inner Circle Trader

Institutional order flow analysis, liquidity sweeps, optimal trade entry (OTE), and market structure alignment. We identify where smart money is positioned before entering.

SMC — Smart Money Concepts

Supply and demand zones, order blocks, breaker blocks, and fair value gaps. Our setups target institutional reference points with high reward-to-risk ratios (typically 2:1+).
