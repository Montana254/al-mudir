1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience. AL-MUDIR uses a combination of cookies and localStorage to operate the Service.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When the TradingView charting widget is loaded, TradingView may store cookies in accordance with their own cookie policy. We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to TradingView's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites.
  • Set preferences for future cookie use.

Please note that disabling essential cookies or clearing localStorage will end your active session and require you to log in again.

5. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser localStorage to persist your session token and theme preference. Unlike cookies, localStorage data is not sent to the server with each request — it is only accessed by client-side JavaScript. You can clear localStorage through your browser's developer tools or settings.
